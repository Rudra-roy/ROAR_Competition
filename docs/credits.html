Credits and licence

Who this entry built on, what it did and did not take from other entries, and the licences of what is on this site.

Credits

The racing line was first built from the line of the Summer 2026 first-place entry (github.com/micahc123/roar2026), itself built on github.com/MightyMark3/ROAR_1_S25. The corner widening used the path that ROAR_1_S25's car drove in our simulator (recorded telemetry) as evidence of where the road is clear. No code from either repository is included. Neither carries a licence, so we credit the authors here and disclose this use to the organizers with the submission. The same was done with the recorded path of the Summer 2026 first-place entry's car in our simulator (telemetry only): it is part of the driven-path evidence for the exit steps of the line (--winner) and the benchmark the lap was compared against section by section. The wall map and the obstacle probe use only our own simulator runs.

ROAR_PY, CARLA and the competition starter code: the ROAR team at UC Berkeley.

This is the Credits section of the README, word for word.

Licence

MIT, see LICENSE. It covers our code and data. `competition_runner.py` and `infrastructure.py` are the ROAR team's unmodified starter files, and the repositories named in Credits carry no licence of their own.

Fonts on this site

Saira, Source Serif 4 and IBM Plex Mono, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, served from this site's own files in the Latin subset cut by Fontsource. Licence texts: Saira, Source Serif 4, IBM Plex Mono.

Data and images on this site

The replays and charts are drawn from the logs of our own races and from the experiment log we kept while building the entry; where a number comes from a source that is not published in the repository, the page says so beside it. The camera images and the video are CARLA 0.9.12 renders of one of our own races. The wall map and the obstacle probe use only our own simulator runs. The pages' own code makes no requests to other servers; anything else a visitor's browser fetches comes from the service this site is served through, not from our files.
